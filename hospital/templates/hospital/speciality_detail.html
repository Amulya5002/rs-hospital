About {{ department.department_name }}

{% if department.department_name == "Cardiology" %}

Cardiology is the branch of medicine concerned with the diagnosis, treatment and prevention of diseases affecting the heart and blood vessels.

The heart plays an important role in circulating blood and supplying oxygen throughout the body. Cardiologists help patients maintain heart health and manage different cardiovascular conditions.

Conditions Treated

  • High blood pressure
  • Coronary artery disease
  • Heart rhythm disorders
  • Heart failure
  • Heart valve diseases
  • Chest pain and related heart conditions

Common Symptoms

  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Dizziness or fainting
  • Unusual tiredness

Diagnostic Tests

  • Electrocardiogram (ECG)
  • Echocardiogram (ECHO)
  • Treadmill Test (TMT)
  • Holter monitoring

Treatment and Care

Cardiology care may include medication, lifestyle guidance, regular heart monitoring and specialised procedures depending on the patient's condition.

{% elif department.department_name == "Neurology" %}

Neurology is the medical speciality focused on the brain, spinal cord and nervous system. Neurologists diagnose and manage conditions that affect movement, memory, sensation and coordination.

Conditions Treated

  • Migraine and recurring headaches
  • Epilepsy and seizures
  • Stroke
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Peripheral nerve disorders
  • Multiple sclerosis

Common Symptoms

  • Frequent or severe headaches
  • Numbness or tingling
  • Muscle weakness
  • Memory problems
  • Balance and coordination difficulties

Diagnostic Tests

  • MRI and CT scans
  • Electroencephalogram (EEG)
  • Nerve conduction studies
  • Neurological examinations

Treatment and Care

Treatment may include medication, neurological monitoring, rehabilitation and specialised procedures based on the diagnosis.

{% elif department.department_name == "Surgical Gastroenterology" %}

Surgical Gastroenterology focuses on the surgical management of diseases affecting the digestive system, including the stomach, intestines, liver, pancreas and gallbladder.

Conditions Treated

  • Gallstones
  • Appendicitis
  • Hernia
  • Digestive tract disorders requiring surgery
  • Selected liver and pancreatic conditions
  • Gastrointestinal tumours

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent abdominal pain
  • Vomiting and nausea
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Changes in bowel habits
  • Unexplained digestive problems

Diagnostic Tests

  • Ultrasound examination
  • Endoscopy
  • Colonoscopy
  • CT and MRI scans

Treatment and Care

Treatment may include medication, surgical procedures, minimally invasive surgery and post-operative care depending on the condition.

{% elif department.department_name == "Urology" %}

Urology is the speciality concerned with the urinary system in men and women and the male reproductive system.

Conditions Treated

  • Kidney stones
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Bladder disorders
  • Prostate enlargement
  • Urinary incontinence
  • Selected kidney and urinary tract conditions

Common Symptoms

  • Pain or burning during urination
  • Frequent urination
  • Blood in urine
  • Difficulty passing urine
  • Lower abdominal or back pain

Diagnostic Tests

  • Urine tests
  • Ultrasound scans
  • CT scans
  • Cystoscopy

Treatment and Care

Urological treatment may include medication, stone management, minimally invasive procedures and surgery when required.

{% elif department.department_name == "Pulmonology" %}

Pulmonology is the branch of medicine that focuses on the lungs and respiratory system. Pulmonologists diagnose and treat conditions that affect breathing and lung function.

Conditions Treated

  • Asthma
  • Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
  • Pneumonia
  • Bronchitis
  • Sleep-related breathing disorders
  • Other respiratory conditions

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent cough
  • Shortness of breath
  • Wheezing
  • Chest tightness
  • Frequent respiratory infections

Diagnostic Tests

  • Pulmonary function tests
  • Chest X-rays
  • CT scans
  • Bronchoscopy

Treatment and Care

Treatment may include inhalers, medication, oxygen therapy when indicated, breathing support and pulmonary rehabilitation.

{% elif department.department_name == "Gynecology" %}

Gynecology focuses on women's reproductive health and the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the female reproductive system.

Conditions Treated

  • Menstrual disorders
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
  • Endometriosis
  • Uterine fibroids
  • Pelvic infections
  • Menopause-related concerns

Common Symptoms

  • Irregular periods
  • Severe menstrual pain
  • Unusual vaginal bleeding
  • Pelvic pain
  • Hormonal changes

Diagnostic Tests

  • Pelvic examination
  • Ultrasound scans
  • Pap smear screening
  • Hormonal blood tests

Treatment and Care

Care may include preventive screening, medication, hormonal treatment, counselling and surgical procedures when needed.

{% elif department.department_name == "Orthopaedic & Trauma" %}

Orthopaedics and Trauma focuses on the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments and other parts of the musculoskeletal system. It also provides care for injuries caused by accidents and physical trauma.

Conditions Treated

  • Bone fractures
  • Arthritis
  • Sports injuries
  • Joint disorders
  • Spine-related conditions
  • Ligament and tendon injuries

Common Symptoms

  • Joint pain and stiffness
  • Back and neck pain
  • Swelling around joints
  • Difficulty walking or moving
  • Muscle and bone injuries

Diagnostic Tests

  • X-rays
  • MRI scans
  • CT scans
  • Bone density tests

Treatment and Care

Treatment may include medication, physiotherapy, fracture management, joint procedures and orthopaedic surgery.

{% elif department.department_name == "Critical Care" %}

Critical Care is a specialised medical service for patients with serious, life-threatening illnesses or injuries who require continuous monitoring and advanced medical support.

Conditions Managed

  • Severe infections and sepsis
  • Serious breathing difficulties
  • Major injuries and trauma
  • Severe heart-related emergencies
  • Organ failure
  • Post-operative critical conditions

When Critical Care Is Needed

  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Unconsciousness
  • Severe injuries
  • Unstable vital signs
  • Need for intensive monitoring

Critical Care Services

  • Intensive care unit monitoring
  • Respiratory support
  • Continuous vital sign monitoring
  • Emergency medical management
  • Multidisciplinary patient care

Treatment and Care

Critical care involves close monitoring, specialised equipment, medicines and coordinated treatment by a medical team.
